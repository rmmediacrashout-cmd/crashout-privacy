Crashout

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 10 May 2025  ·  Last Updated: 10 May 2025

1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Crashout ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app. Crashout is designed so that most gameplay information stays on your device and is used only to run the game experience. This policy applies to all users of the Crashout app available on the Apple App Store.

2. Data Stored Only on Your Device

Crashout stores the following information locally on your device: your chosen display name, the locally generated account code shown in Profile Settings, game setup choices, haptics preferences, session progress, XP, streaks, recap information and photos you choose to save to the Session Gallery. This information is not uploaded to Crashout servers as part of normal gameplay.

3. Camera and Photos

If you use the camera feature, Crashout requests camera access so you can capture photos during a session. Photos are saved only inside the app on your device when you choose to keep them. Crashout does not upload session photos to a remote server.

4. Feedback and Challenge Preferences

Crashout may transmit information that you actively choose to share with us, including feedback messages, optional contact details you include in feedback, and signals about whether you liked or disliked a challenge. We use this information to understand bugs, improve gameplay, evaluate challenge quality and adjust future challenge content.

5. Usage Analytics

Crashout may also collect or transmit limited usage information about how the app is used, such as session duration, feature interactions, app open frequency, challenge flow behavior and other product performance signals. We use this information to understand how long the app is played, improve app stability, refine the game flow and make product decisions.

6. No In-App Purchases and No Advertising Tracking

Crashout does not currently process in-app purchases. Crashout also does not use third-party advertising SDKs or tracking technologies for targeted advertising.

7. Third-Party Services

Crashout uses Apple's standard infrastructure (App Store, TestFlight) to distribute the app. Apple may collect certain diagnostic and usage data as described in Apple's own Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/privacy/). Crashout does not integrate any other third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs at this time.

8. Data Retention

Locally stored gameplay data and photos remain on your device until you delete them in the app, overwrite them through normal usage, or remove the app from your device. Feedback messages, optional contact details, challenge preference signals and usage analytics sent to Crashout may be retained for product support, quality review, moderation, measurement and feature planning. We retain transmitted data only as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purposes.

9. Sharing of Information

Crashout does not sell your personal information. Information stored locally on your device is not shared with third parties as part of normal gameplay. If you choose to use Apple's share sheet, external apps or services may handle the shared content under their own privacy policies.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information handled by the app. Local data is stored within the app's sandboxed environment as provided by iOS. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you. Since the majority of data is stored locally on your device, you can manage or delete it at any time by clearing your data within the app or by deleting the app. For any data transmitted to us (feedback, preferences, analytics), you may contact us to request access, correction or deletion.

12. Children and Age-Restricted Use

Crashout is not directed at children under the age of 17. Some challenge modes may include alcohol-related or mature social content. Crashout should only be used by people who are legally allowed to participate in the selected type of activity in their region. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. International Users

If you are accessing Crashout from outside Germany, please be aware that any information transmitted to us may be processed in Germany or other locations where our service providers operate.

14. Changes to This Policy

If Crashout's data practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated before the new collection or use begins. The updated policy will be made available both within the app and at the URL where this policy is hosted. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
